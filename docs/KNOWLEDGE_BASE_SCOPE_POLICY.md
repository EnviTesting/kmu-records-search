# Knowledge base scope policy

## Geographic scope

The Knowledge Records Search Tool is centred on Trinidad and Tobago. A record may be hosted outside Trinidad and Tobago, but it must materially describe, regulate, report on, designate, analyse or provide data relevant to Trinidad and Tobago.

## Source scope

EMA records form the core collection. Related government, institutional, parliamentary, statistical, regional and international sources are separately attributed and remain distinguishable from EMA-held records. Media coverage and Data Search catalogue entries are separate discovery collections and are not counted as EMA knowledge records.

## Interpretation safeguard

Indexing a record, institution, place, dataset or media story does not establish statutory responsibility, legal applicability, environmental condition, compliance status, hazard extent, causation or an EMA determination. Original authoritative sources should be consulted for formal reliance.
