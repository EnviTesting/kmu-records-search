# Knowledge base Scope Policy

## Geographic boundary

The EMA Document Search Tool is hard-restricted to **Trinidad and Tobago**. A record may be hosted outside Trinidad and Tobago, but it must materially describe, regulate, report on, designate, analyse or provide data for Trinidad and Tobago.

## Core source families

- EMA records and publications
- Courts and Environmental Commission proceedings
- Parliament of Trinidad and Tobago evidence and oversight
- Trinidad and Tobago Government Gazette and Legal Notices
- Trinidad and Tobago Government policies, strategies and agency records

## Contextual source families

- Research and statistical records with a material environmental-management relationship
- International repositories containing Trinidad and Tobago national submissions, country profiles, country projects, country sites or country datasets
- Verified audiovisual records from EMA, Parliament or Trinidad and Tobago-specific environmental projects

## International inclusion test

An international record must identify Trinidad and Tobago as at least one of the following:

- reporting country / national submission;
- subject country / country profile;
- project country;
- designated site located in Trinidad and Tobago; or
- named country dataset or national geographic unit.

Generic Caribbean, SIDS, regional or global material is excluded unless the indexed record contains a distinct Trinidad and Tobago-specific component that can stand on its own.

## Deduplication

The same intellectual document should not appear as separate search results solely because multiple repositories host copies. Prefer one primary record and retain alternate official repository links where practical.

## Interpretation safeguards

Knowledge base presence or record counts do not measure environmental importance, environmental condition, regulatory performance or institutional effectiveness. Research/statistical records are contextual evidence. Video is supplementary media and does not replace published legislation, policy, reports or submissions.

## Supplementary discovery layers

`environmental_observances.json` and `spatial_preview.json` support navigation and discovery but are not counted as document knowledge-base records. Environmental observances are guided by EMA's own Environmental Calendar; international links are labelled according to whether they are UN/treaty custodians, regional institutional references, or non-UN campaign stewards. Spatial preview coordinates are representative only and must not be represented as EMA legal, regulatory, survey or GIS boundaries. Administrative-area shading is derived from explicit indexed place-name matches and must not be treated as a project boundary, jurisdiction, environmental-condition rating, compliance status or EMA determination.

## News and media

Official EMA News & Events remain indexed in the main search. The separate EMA in the News page uses a public-safe external media register and remains distinguishable from EMA-authored communications. Archived clipping/OCR-only records and EMA-unit/manager mappings are excluded from that public media dataset. Media volume is not a measure of environmental incidence, severity, compliance status or environmental condition.
