How to use this page

A large category may reflect strong documentation, a heavily represented regulatory programme, or simply better harvesting. A small category may indicate a genuine collection gap, different terminology, records held by another institution, weak metadata, or material that has not yet been digitised. Investigate before drawing conclusions.
