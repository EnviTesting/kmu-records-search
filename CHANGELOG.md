# Changelog

## 6.2.1 — 2026-08-17

Corpus version: **2026.08.17.1** (unchanged)

- Renamed the public-facing product to **EMA Document Search Tool**.
- Updated the Knowledge Management contact to `rseemungal@ema.co.tt`.
